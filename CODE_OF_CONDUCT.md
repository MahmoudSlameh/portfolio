# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its Code of Conduct.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/MahmoudSlameh). All reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow
this Code of Conduct, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
